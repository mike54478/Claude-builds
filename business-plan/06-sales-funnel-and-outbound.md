# Sales Funnel & Outbound Campaigns

## The funnel

```
Lead list (mystery-shop audit + scraped Google Maps, FSM-qualified)
   │
   ├── Cold call  ──┐
   ├── Cold email ──┼──→  15-min demo call  ──→  14-day free trial  ──→  Day-10 ROI review  ──→  PAID
   └── FB groups  ──┘      (live demo line,        (their real             ("FirstRing booked      (push annual:
        & referrals         missed-call audit       after-hours line,       7 jobs ≈ $3,100.        2 months free)
                            results)                we set everything up)   Keep it?")
```

**Why this shape:** the trial is the close. Once their real line forwards to FirstRing and the owner
gets "BOOKED — $450" texts at 9pm, the day-10 call isn't a sales call — it's reading their own
revenue back to them. Every funnel stage exists to get the forwarding code dialed.

**Funnel principles**
- Founder sells everything until $10k MRR. No SDRs, no agencies (they'd burn the lead list).
- Demo ≤15 minutes, on the phone (not Zoom — this buyer lives on the phone). Core move: "Call this
  number right now and pretend your water heater burst." Let them try to break it live.
- Every demo no-show gets called twice, then dropped into the email nurture. Trades no-show ~40%;
  it's not rejection, it's a slammed schedule. Same-day or next-day demos only — never book a week out.
- Exit every stage with a scheduled next step on the calendar before hanging up.

## Lead generation ($0 budget)

1. **Mystery-shop audit list (highest intent):** every shop that missed our week-1 audit call is a
   lead with personalized, undeniable proof. Refresh continuously: audit 50 new shops/week in
   target metros (a $6/hr VA does this from month 2; founder does weeks 1–4).
2. **Scraped Google Maps:** plumbing/HVAC in 3 launch metros, 10–200 reviews (proxy for 5–50
   employees), has a website. Qualify FSM usage from "book online" buttons (HCP/Jobber booking
   widgets are identifiable in page source — scriptable). Target: 2,000-lead base, +250/week.
3. **Facebook groups** (HVAC/plumbing owner groups — large and highly active) + r/HVAC, r/Plumbing
   business threads: value-first posts, never pitch-first (Campaign D).
4. **Referrals & local clusters:** every closed customer asked for 2 intros at the day-30 mark
   (see growth loops).

## Campaign A — Cold calls (primary channel; founder, 30/day, 5 days/week)

Call at 7:30–8:30am or 4:30–6pm (owners answer outside dispatch hours). Mobile numbers from the
business listing where possible.

**Opener (missed-call proof variant — audit-list leads):**
> "Hey [Name], this is [Founder] — quick confession: I called your shop Tuesday at 2:15 and it rang
> through to voicemail. I'm not mad, I'm calling because I counted — that happens to 6 out of 10
> shops I call, and every one of those is usually a customer calling the next plumber on Google.
> Got 30 seconds for what I do about it?"

**Pitch (15 seconds):**
> "FirstRing answers the calls you miss — nights, weekends, when the line's tied up. It talks to the
> customer like a good CSR and books the job straight into [Housecall Pro]. You get a text with the
> recording 30 seconds later. Costs less than an answering service, and an answering service
> doesn't book anything."

**The close (always the demo line, not a meeting):**
> "Don't take my word — got a pen? Call this number right now after we hang up and pretend your
> water heater's flooding the garage: (480) 555-0142. If it handles you, I'll set the whole thing
> up free on your after-hours line for two weeks. When's good for a 15-minute setup call — tomorrow
> morning or afternoon?"

**Objection handling:** use the battlecard in `02-competitive-landscape.md`. Voicemail script: 20
seconds, mention the Tuesday missed call, leave demo-line number, "I'll text you the details."
**Always follow every dial with the SMS** (below) — trades read texts, not email.

**Follow-up SMS (immediately after any dial):**
> "[Name] — [Founder] from FirstRing. We answer plumbing & HVAC phones after hours and book the jobs
> into Housecall Pro/Jobber. Hear it yourself: (480) 555-0142 (try to stump it). Free 2-week trial,
> we set it up. Worth 15 min?"

## Campaign B — Cold email (parallel air cover; 50/day/inbox, 3 inboxes warmed from week 1)

4-touch sequence, plain text, sent from "[Founder] from FirstRing," heavy personalization from the
audit + their Google reviews.

**Email 1 — the receipt:**
Subject: `called you Tuesday at 2:15pm`
> [Name] — I called [Company] Tuesday at 2:15pm with a water-heater question. Rang out to voicemail.
> No judgment — we audited 100 [metro] plumbing companies and 62 did the same thing. The problem is
> that caller usually just dials the next company on Google. I built FirstRing to fix exactly this:
> it answers the calls you miss, handles the customer like a good CSR, and books the job into
> [their FSM]. You get a text with the recording 30 seconds later.
> Hear it work — call (480) 555-0142 and pretend you've got a burst pipe.
> Want me to run it free on your after-hours line for two weeks? Setup takes one 30-min call. — [Founder]

**Email 2 (day 3) — the math:**
Subject: `$20,800/yr`
> One missed call a week × $400 average ticket = $20,800 a year going to the shop down the street.
> That's the boring version. The expensive version is the 11pm no-heat call in January that was a
> $7k changeout. FirstRing starts at $199/mo and books jobs while you sleep. Two-week trial's free —
> reply "audit" and I'll run a missed-call audit on your line first so you can see the damage.

**Email 3 (day 7) — proof:** 90-second recording of a real (anonymized) 1am emergency call +
founding-customer quote + "we set everything up, you change nothing."

**Email 4 (day 12) — breakup:**
> Last one from me. If missed calls aren't costing you anything, ignore this and I'll close the
> file. If you're not sure, that's exactly what the free audit is for — reply "audit" and you'll
> have the numbers by Friday either way.

## Campaign C — "Free Missed-Call Audit" (the lead magnet that runs itself)

Productize week 1's validation tactic: prospects opt in → we (legally, on our own outbound test
calls + their call-forwarding stats) measure their missed-call rate for 7 days → deliver a 1-page
PDF: calls missed, estimated dollars lost, benchmark vs. metro average. Ends with one line: "Want
these answered next week?" Low-friction CTA for every channel; converts skeptics with their own data.

## Campaign D — Facebook groups & communities (2 posts/week, zero pitch)

Value posts in owner groups: the audit data ("We called 100 shops in Phoenix — here's the missed-call
scoreboard"), call-handling teardowns ("what your answering service said vs. what books a job"),
storm-week prep checklists. Answer every comment. Pitch only in DMs when asked. This channel
compounds: by month 3 it produces inbound demos and it's where screenshots of ROI texts get shared
(see growth loops).
