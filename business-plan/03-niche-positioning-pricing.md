# Niche, Positioning, and Pricing

## The niche (precisely)

**Who:** US residential plumbing and HVAC companies, 5–50 employees, running Housecall Pro, Jobber,
or ServiceTitan, doing service work (not new-construction only).

**Why exactly this slice:**
- **Plumbing + HVAC, not "home services":** highest emergency-call rate (burst pipes, no-heat,
  no-cool = after-hours revenue), highest average tickets, most acute pain. Electricians, garage
  doors, etc. come later — same product, sequenced expansion (see roadmap).
- **5–50 employees:** below 5, they can't afford $199 reliably and churn with the business; above
  50, they have call centers, procurement, and funded competitors courting them. 5–50 means real
  call volume, real money lost, no committee.
- **Must use FSM software:** "books into your existing system" is our killer feature, and FSM usage
  signals a business organized enough to value bookings. Also makes leads easy to qualify.
- **Start in 3 metros, not nationwide** (pick by trade density + my timezone, e.g., Phoenix,
  Dallas, Tampa): reference customers cluster locally — plumbers know plumbers through supply
  houses and trade groups, and "I'm the company that answers for [known local shop]" closes deals.

**The wedge use-case:** after-hours + overflow answering. We are NOT asking anyone to fire their
CSR or change their number. Conditional call forwarding (no answer in ~20 seconds → FirstRing) means
zero switching cost and zero risk. Land on the missed calls; expand to 24/7 once trusted.

## Brand & message

- **Name:** FirstRing — "answered on the first ring."
- **Tagline:** *Never lose another job to a missed call.*
- **Message hierarchy:** 1) Revenue saved (jobs booked, in dollars) → 2) Always-on (2am, holidays,
  storm surges) → 3) Zero effort (live in 24h, we set it up, keep your number) → 4) Cheaper than an
  answering service that only takes messages.
- **Voice:** plainspoken, trade-respecting, zero AI hype. We never say "revolutionary AI." We say
  "it answers your phone and books the job."

## Pricing & packaging

Anchored against answering services ($250–$700/mo) and one saved job (~$400). Per-location pricing,
minutes-metered to protect margin (see `08-financial-model.md` for COGS).

| | **After-Hours** $199/mo | **24/7 Pro** $399/mo | **Multi-Truck** $799/mo |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coverage | Nights, weekends, holidays | 24/7 overflow + after-hours | 24/7, multiple lines/locations |
| Included minutes | 150/mo | 400/mo | 1,000/mo |
| Emergency triage + warm transfer to on-call tech | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Books into Housecall Pro / Jobber | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| ServiceTitan integration | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| Missed-call text-back (instant SMS to callers who hang up) | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| Owner real-time SMS summaries + weekly ROI report | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Spanish-language calls | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| Custom call flows & priority support (founder's cell) | — | — | ✓ |
| Overage | $0.45/min | $0.40/min | $0.35/min |

**Mechanics:**
- **14-day free trial** on the After-Hours plan — their real line, real calls, no card required for
  design partners / card-up-front once funnel matures. The trial IS the close: the day-10 ROI call
  shows jobs booked in dollars.
- **Founding-customer rate:** first 20 customers get 24/7 Pro at the $199 price, locked for life,
  in exchange for a testimonial and review-call access. (Fills the logo wall and reference pool.)
- **Annual prepay = 2 months free**, pushed at every close. Cash up front funds the company
  (replaces venture capital) and pre-kills seasonal churn.
- **No contracts on monthly.** Confidence is the brand. Cancellation requires a 5-minute call —
  which is a save opportunity (downgrade to After-Hours, pause for season).
- **No setup fee** under 10 trucks. White-glove setup is our differentiator, not an upsell.

**Blended ARPU assumption:** mix of ~45% After-Hours, ~45% Pro, ~10% Multi-Truck plus overages →
**~$280/customer/mo**. $10k MRR ≈ **36–38 customers**.

**Price-integrity rules:** never discount monthly price (only add free months or extend trials);
raise list prices for new customers once we pass 25 logos (founding customers keep theirs — fuels
referral urgency: "get in before the price goes up").
