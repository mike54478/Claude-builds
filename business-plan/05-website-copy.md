## FAQ

**Do I have to change my phone number?** No. Your number, your carrier. We only get the calls you
don't answer — or take the lines fully off your hands after hours. You can turn it off anytime.

**What does it book jobs into?** Housecall Pro, Jobber, and ServiceTitan today. No FSM? It books to
a calendar and texts you a dispatch sheet.

**What about real emergencies?** You set the rules: what counts as an emergency, who's on call,
what the emergency fee is. FirstRing triages, captures the address first, and patches your tech in.
If the tech doesn't pick up, it escalates down your list and texts everyone the details.

**What if it doesn't know the answer?** It takes a detailed message and tells the caller exactly
when to expect a callback — it never bluffs. You see flagged calls immediately.

**How long does setup take?** One 30-minute call with us. You're live the same day. We build your
call flows, connect your software, and test it with you before a single customer hears it.

**Is there a contract?** No. Monthly, cancel anytime. We keep customers by booking them jobs, not
with paperwork.
