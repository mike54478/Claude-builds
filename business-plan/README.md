# FirstRing — $0 → $10k MRR in 180 Days

**The bet:** an AI phone receptionist for plumbing & HVAC companies (5–50 employees) that answers
the calls they miss, triages emergencies to the on-call tech, and books jobs directly into
Housecall Pro / Jobber / ServiceTitan.

**The math:** ~38 customers × ~$280 blended ARPU = $10.4k MRR by day 182, at ~88% gross margin,
with <$1,000 of cash spent before first revenue.

**Why this wins** (full defense in `00-thesis.md`): it replaces money already being spent on human
answering services with a product that books jobs instead of taking messages; ROI is provable in
the customer's own dollars inside a 14-day free trial; the buyer is reachable by cold phone call
for $0; and one technical founder can ship the MVP in 3 weeks.
