# The 180-Day Plan — Weekly Milestones

Operating rhythm every week, non-negotiable: **Mon–Thu = sell mornings (7:30–9am dials), build
midday, sell evenings (4:30–6pm dials). Friday = QA every flagged call, ship fixes, update the
model with actuals, plan next week.** Metrics reviewed weekly: dials, demos held, trials started,
trial→paid, MRR, churn, % trials with a booked job in 7 days.

## Contingency triggers (pre-committed, so they're decisions, not debates)

- **Week 2 — validation fails kill criteria** → pivot vertical with the same product (dental, med
  spa, auto repair) or to agency white-label; lose 2 weeks, not the company.
- **Week 9 — trial→paid <40%** → freeze outbound scaling; founder QAs every call live for 2 weeks;
  fix the top 3 failure modes; re-measure. Selling a leaky product to a word-of-mouth market is fatal.
- **Week 12 — MRR <$2,500** → funnel diagnosis: if demos <4/wk it's an activity problem (raise dials,
  add metros); if demos fine but closes low it's a pitch/proof problem (more recordings, audit-first
  selling). Do not add new channels until the diagnosis is written down.
- **Week 20 — MRR <$6,000** → extend timeline honestly; cut any spend not directly producing demos;
  consider founding-rate promo blitz in FB groups. The model says month 8 at worst-case rates — the
  business survives; the deadline is the only casualty.
