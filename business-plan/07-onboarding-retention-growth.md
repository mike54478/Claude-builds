# Onboarding, Retention, and Growth Loops

## Onboarding workflow — "Live in 24 hours"

White-glove, founder-run for the first ~50 customers. Onboarding is the product demo, the trust
builder, and the moat the horizontal competitors can't afford at this price point.

**Activation metric (the number the whole company optimizes): first job booked within 7 days of
go-live.** A customer who sees a booked job in week one effectively never churns; one who doesn't is
a churn risk flagged for intervention.

### T+0 — Kickoff call (30 min, scheduled at the close)
1. Intake (live, founder fills the form — never send the owner a form): services offered, service
   area zips, dispatch/emergency fees, price ranges we may quote, on-call rotation + escalation
   order, FSM login (HCP/Jobber OAuth connect on the call), greeting preferences, no-go topics.
2. Set expectations: what FirstRing will and won't say; how transfers work; where the texts come from.
3. Schedule go-live (same day or next morning).

### T+2–6 hours — Build & internal test
Configure flows from the trade template; founder places 5 test calls covering: routine booking,
emergency triage + warm transfer (to founder's phone first, then to their on-call tech), price
question, angry caller, out-of-area caller. Fix, re-test.

### T+24h — Go-live call (15 min)
- Owner calls the line and role-plays a customer (they always try to break it; let them — this is
  where trust is won).
- Dial the carrier forwarding codes together (no-answer/busy → FirstRing; after-hours schedule set).
- Confirm owner + ops manager get the SMS summaries. Send the "you're live" text.

### Days 1–7 — Guarded launch
- **Founder reviews every single call recording** within hours; fixes flow issues same-day; texts
  the owner proactively about anything imperfect *before they notice* ("Caller #6 asked about water
  softeners — I've taught it your answer, here's the clip"). This proactive-confession move builds
  more trust than perfection would.
- Day-3 text check-in. Day-7 call: review the call log, celebrate the first booked jobs in dollars.

### Day 10 (trial) — ROI review = the close
One page: calls answered, jobs booked, estimated revenue, the best recording. "FirstRing booked
$3,100 in 10 days. The plan that did it is $199/mo — and annual gets you 2 months free. Keep it on?"

### Day 30 — First monthly report + referral ask (see growth loops).

## Retention system (target: <3% gross monthly churn)

**1. The product reports its own ROI, constantly.**
- Real-time "BOOKED — $450" texts (dopamine, attributable, screenshot-able).
- Weekly ROI email; monthly one-page report with a cumulative "revenue saved since joining" number
  designed to be shown to a spouse or business partner.

**2. Quality assurance as a ritual.** Flagged/low-confidence calls reviewed within 24h forever, not
just week one. Every flow improvement proactively announced ("FirstRing now handles X"). Monthly
changelog text. The customer should feel the product getting smarter every month.

**3. Failure protocol (voice AI WILL have bad calls).** A botched call surfaced by us first + fixed
same-day + a free month if it plausibly cost a job. One sincere save creates a reference customer;
one hidden failure creates churn and a bad name in a town where all the plumbers know each other.

**4. Structural retention.**
- Annual prepay (2 months free) pushed at close and at every renewal touchpoint.
- Seasonal **pause** (drop to After-Hours $199 or $49 hibernate) instead of cancel — HVAC slow
  seasons are the #1 predictable churn driver; never make them re-onboard.
- Cancellation = 5-minute call: diagnose, offer downgrade/pause, learn. Target 30% save rate.

**5. Expansion = retention.** Month-4+ features (Spanish, missed-call text-back, storm-surge mode,
outbound recalls) deepen dependence. A customer using 3 features churns at a fraction of the rate
of one using 1.

**Churn early-warning dashboard:** no booked job in 14 days · owner stopped opening ROI emails ·
call volume down >40% MoM · forwarding turned off (instant alert — that's a cancellation in progress).
Any trigger → founder calls within 24h.

## Growth loops (compounding > campaigns)

**Loop 1 — The audit flywheel.** Mystery-shop audits → missed-call data → (a) personalized outbound
hooks, (b) "State of the Missed Call in [Metro]" content that trade groups share, (c) the prospects
audited become the lead list. Every week of selling produces next week's leads and content.

**Loop 2 — The screenshot loop.** The "BOOKED — $450 at 2am" owner text is engineered to be bragged
about — owners post these in the same Facebook groups we prospect in. Make it beautiful, branded,
and include "via FirstRing." Monthly reports likewise. Trades trust other trades; this is our
word-of-mouth engine made tangible.

**Loop 3 — Referral program (month 2+).** Give a month, get a month — but the real mechanism is the
day-30 ask: "Who are the two best shops you know that aren't your competitors?" Plumbers refer
HVAC and vice versa (non-competing, same pain). Target: 25% of customers produce ≥1 referral;
referral closes at 3× cold rate. Supply houses and BNI chapters amplify locally.

**Loop 4 — Marketplace distribution (month 5+).** Jobber and Housecall Pro app-marketplace listings:
their SMB customers searching "phone/answering" find us in-context, pre-integrated. Platform
marketplaces are how the platform-native-AI threat gets converted into a channel.

**Loop 5 — Channel partners (month 6+).** Marketing agencies serving home services ("we get you
leads") have a hole: their clients miss the calls the ads generate. FirstRing makes their ad ROI
look better. 20% recurring rev-share white-label/referral tier. One good agency = 5–10 customers/yr
with zero founder selling. (Also: bookkeepers, FSM-implementation consultants.)

**Loop 6 — Content from calls (ongoing).** Anonymized real recordings — the 1am save, the angry
caller defused — as 60-second clips for FB groups/YouTube/TikTok. The product manufactures its own
marketing material on every shift.
